Privacy Policy for Word Search Legends

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

OcenSoft ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Word Search Legends mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data and privacy in relation to your use of the App. Please read it carefully to understand our views and practices regarding your data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Word Search Legends is designed to be an offline-first mobile application.

Personal Data: We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or contact lists).
Local Storage: All gameplay data (including completed levels, unlocked stars, streaks, and settings) is stored entirely locally on your device using encrypted, sandbox storage (react-native-mmkv). This data never leaves your device and cannot be accessed by us or any third party.
2. Third-Party Services (Ad Advertising)

To support the free operation of the App, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect and process device-specific information to serve, personalize, and measure advertisements. This data may include:

Mobile Advertising Identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA).
Device characteristics (model, OS version, language settings).
IP addresses (used to determine general geographic location).

For more information on how Google handles and protects your data, please read the Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Children's Privacy

Our App does not collect any personal data. We do not knowingly target or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to a third-party service through our App, please contact us, and we will assist in resolving the issue.

4. GDPR (European Union) and CCPA (California) Compliance

Since the App does not collect, store, or transmit any user data to our servers, we are fully compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) by design.

You do not need to request data deletion because no data is retained on our servers.
If you wish to delete all game progress, you can simply uninstall the App or clear the App's data in your device settings.
5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
